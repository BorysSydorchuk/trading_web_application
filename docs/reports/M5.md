## What was built

- **Pure sizing core** (`backend/app/domain/position_size.py`, M5-01/02): Decimal only, lot rounded **down** to the volume step, never rounded up to the minimum (refuses with `below_min_volume`), real risk ≤ budget, SL by price or points (broker `point`), TP for RR 1.5 / 2 / 3 rounded away from entry to the tick grid, bounded Decimal input. Stable error codes (`PositionSizeError`). 97 % coverage.
- **Broker data** (M5-03): `symbol_specs.point` and `tick_value_loss` captured from MT5 (never inferred). Margin requests table.
- **Schema clean-up** (M5-04b): dropped `calculator_margin_samples` and the `signature` columns (PM scope check).
- **API** (M5-04c/d, contract in docs/api.md "## M5"):
  - `GET /api/accounts/{id}/symbol-specs`, `POST /api/calculator/calculate`: answers from synced specs, never touches MT5.
  - Margin: `POST/GET /api/calculator/margin-requests`; worker job every 2 s does a read-only `order_calc_margin` on the account's terminal, sharing one MT5 source and lock with sync. 60 s expiry. Offline: latest live margin for the same account/symbol/direction scaled to the volume, shown as a dated estimate; else «маржа недоступна» (never zero).
- **UI** (M5-05): «Калькулятор» page and a compact «Калькулятор позиції» panel on «Головна» (same component), Ukrainian, all error codes shown.
- **QA tooling** (M5-06): `scripts/qa_live_check_m5.py` (isolated QA DB `trading_journal_qa_m5`, fixture MT5, AI off), synthetic fixture `calculator_qa.json`.
- **Canonical symbols** (M5-08, owner finding): `config/symbols.yaml`; FundedNext's `GER30` is stored and shown as `GER40`, shown as «GER40 (GER30)» in the calculator; broker names only in MT5 calls.
- **Cross-currency loss value** (M5-09, owner finding GER40 FAIL): EUR-profit symbols on a USD account size with `tick_size × contract_size × EURUSD ask` (table `fx_rates`, synced every cycle) checked against MT5 `order_calc_profit` (read-only); MT5's stale `tick_value` 0.12 is no longer used. Rate and its time shown under the result.
- **Risk base = account size** (M5-10, PM decision): every percentage (calculator, trade risk/P&L %, daily/weekly stop rules) is of «Розмір рахунку»; current balance is information only.
- **Fast checks** (M5-11): change tiers, pytest-xdist with one test DB per worker; `make check` 844 s → 93 s, no dev DB migration.

## How to run

- Normal app: `start.bat` (or `make dev`), menu «Калькулятор», or the panel on «Головна».
- Live margin and the EUR→USD rate need the worker and the MT5 terminal of that account; without them sizing still works (rate shown with its time, stale warning after 1 h), margin shows the estimate or «маржа недоступна».
- Isolated QA: `python scripts\qa_live_check_m5.py` (see docs/reports/M5-live-check.md), stop with `stop.bat`.
