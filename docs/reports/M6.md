## What was built

- **Digest metrics** (M6-02, pure `backend/app/domain/digest_metrics.py`): trades,
  wins/losses/breakeven, net P&L money and % (sum of the sizes of the accounts active in the
  period), total R with n, win rate, avg win/loss R, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown,
  best/worst trade; breakdowns; rules and bias sections; norm (rolling 60 trading days,
  per account and combined, ≥ 20 trading days of history, ±1 SD from `rules.yaml`); chart data.
  Breakeven = |R| < `breakeven_r_threshold` (0.1, `rules.yaml`), else P&L sign (M6-06c).
- **Dashboard API + Home** (M6-03, M6-05): today/week P&L per account vs the stop limits
  (sticky extremes shared with the rules engine), «СТОП на сьогодні/тиждень» banner, open
  positions, inbox counts, sync status, quick links, digest reminder.
- **Digests API** (M6-04, M6-04b, M6-06a): create/regenerate, list with virtual rows for
  periods with trades and no digest, review with `reviewed_at` (migration `cdb0477c3b70`),
  reminder only for the latest due unreviewed week/month.
- **Дайджести UI** (M6-06, M6-06c): list with badges, digest page with red/green vs norm,
  breakdowns with «заповнено N/M», rules, bias daily/weekly/monthly, SVG charts, review and
  next-period bias on one screen, «Сума R» n/N link to the Journal «Без R» filter.
- **AI summary** (M6-07): «Підсумок від ШІ» on button press only, `ANTHROPIC_MODEL_SUMMARY`,
  3–7 factual observations, journal texts treated as data.

## Known issues / limits

- "No advice" in the AI summary is enforced by the prompt only (PM content check open). The first
  live summary took ~10 s, so the 28 s deadline has margin for a week; a long month is not measured.
- Digests created before the breakeven rule keep their old counts until «Оновити».
- The real history is shorter than 20 trading days, so real digests show «замало історії для
  норми» for now; highlighting is proven on synthetic data (M6-08).
- BACKLOG: same-date weekly/monthly sort key and tests (M6-06a review); duplicate `utc_now`
  dependency (M6-03 review).
- Dashboard today/week P&L counts every closed trade of the Brussels day/week, also one with a
  close time after "now". Unreachable with real data (a close is never in the future); seen only
  with the pinned QA clock (M6-08).
- Pre-migration backups keep the newest 10 across all databases: a QA DB migration takes one of
  the 10 slots of the dev DB's safety net (M6-08: `backups/pre-migration-cdb0477c3b70-20261114T165148Z.dump`).
  Already in BACKLOG; worth a decision before M7 (separate QA folder or no dump for `_qa_` DBs).
- Home: «−270.00 USD» wraps onto two lines in the account card at 1280 px (cosmetic, BACKLOG).
- Services with their own clock default (journal requirements, AI timing) are not pinned by the QA
  clock; QA results do not depend on them.
- QA deviations from the plan: own ports 8020/5183, no worker (sync run per account by `--seed`),
  pinned clock file for the QA API; the QA fixture has no week without trades, so "trade-less
  weeks are not listed" is covered by unit tests only.
