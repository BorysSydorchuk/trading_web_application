## What was built

- **Digest metrics** (M6-02, pure `backend/app/domain/digest_metrics.py`): trades,
  wins/losses/breakeven, net P&L money and % (sum of the sizes of the accounts active in the
  period), total R with n, win rate, avg win/loss R, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown,
  best/worst trade; breakdowns; rules and bias sections; norm (rolling 60 trading days,
  per account and combined, ≥ 20 trading days of history, ±1 SD from `rules.yaml`); chart data.
  Breakeven = |R| < `breakeven_r_threshold` (0.1, `rules.yaml`), else P&L sign (M6-06c).
- **Dashboard API + Home** (M6-03, M6-05): today/week P&L per account vs the stop limits
  (sticky extremes shared with the rules engine), «СТОП на сьогодні/тиждень» banner, open
  positions, inbox counts, sync status, quick links, digest reminder.
- **Digests API** (M6-04, M6-04b, M6-06a): create/regenerate, list with virtual rows for
  periods with trades and no digest, review with `reviewed_at` (migration `cdb0477c3b70`),
  reminder only for the latest due unreviewed week/month.
- **Дайджести UI** (M6-06, M6-06c): list with badges, digest page with red/green vs norm,
  breakdowns with «заповнено N/M», rules, bias daily/weekly/monthly, SVG charts, review and
  next-period bias on one screen, «Сума R» n/N link to the Journal «Без R» filter.
- **AI summary** (M6-07): «Підсумок від ШІ» on button press only, `ANTHROPIC_MODEL_SUMMARY`,
  3–7 factual observations, journal texts treated as data.
