## What changed

**All pages:**
- Page titles in Manrope.
- White studio cards; dashed empty and error boxes.
- Muted text uses the AA token. Before, the placeholders used muted at 60 %, which fails contrast.
- Spinners and transitions stop under reduced motion.
- No `dark:` classes are left.

**Journal and «Потрібен опис»:**
- The table is a card with a quiet header and equal row heights.
- Status is a pill: accent for open / needs SL / needs description, neutral for draft / complete.
- At 1440 every column fits. At 1280 the table scrolls sideways, and the «…» column stays pinned.

**Describe:**
- Summary, input, form and save bar are cards.
- The «ШІ» and «MT5» marks are lavender pills.

**Settings:**
- Each block is a section header plus a card.
- Account status is a pill.
- The checkboxes are lavender.

**Bias:**
- Filters and choice buttons are pills, still buttons with `aria-pressed`.
- Note cards and the save bar are studio cards.

**Digests:**
- Metric blocks, bias cards and each chart are studio cards.
- Section headers are in Manrope.
- The stale AI summary note uses the warning colour, because it is a system state.
- Every number stays exact.

**Calculator:**
- The form and the result are cards.
- The lot is shown large and stays exact.
- The targets sit on a tile.
