# M8-01: Exclude a trade from strategy statistics (tier L)

Branch `m8/exclude-from-stats`, commits `f2a3a19` (feature), `6ba0c6b` (review fixes), `55eabba` (test wait budgets). Merge `69b3239` (range `b8d3553..69b3239`).

## What was built
- Action «Не враховувати у статистиці» with a mandatory reason (1–1000 chars, trimmed). It is in the Journal row menu «⋯» (last column) and in a panel at the top of the Describe screen. «Повернути у статистику» reverts it; the reason is optional there.
- History of both actions (when, reason): the `trade_stats_exclusions` table. It is shown on the Describe panel under «Історія».
- Journal: a «виключено» badge with the reason on hover, and a filter «Показати виключені». The filter is on by default; when off, the URL carries `excluded=hide` and the API gets `excluded=false`.
- Leave strategy statistics:
  - the inbox counts and badge, and the inbox list (for «Потрібен опис» and «Чернетки»);
  - every digest number: combined and per-account metrics, the 60-day norm window, breakdowns, the rules section, the bias section, charts, and `trade_ids` (the AI summary input);
  - the digest list rows.
- Stay in money and risk:
  - Home today/week P&L, limit states and «СТОП» banners;
  - the rules engine (1 %, daily and weekly stops, 10 trades/week, re-entry);
  - account balance. The excluded trade keeps its own violations.
- The digest has a block «Виключені угоди: N, P&L X USD». It lists date, account, symbol, P&L and reason, and shows «P&L рахунку разом з виключеними». Strategy P&L + excluded P&L = account P&L.
- Raw MT5 data is untouched. The sync never writes the new columns, so the flag survives re-sync and partial-close updates.
- API: `POST /api/trades/{id}/exclude`, `POST /api/trades/{id}/include`, `GET /api/trades?excluded=`. `TradeRow` and `TradeDetail` have new fields (docs/api.md "M8-01").

## Known issues / assumptions
- A digest that is already saved does not change on exclude/include until «Оновити» is pressed. The dialog says this (DECISIONS 2026-11-18). **Open question for the PM:** should exclude/include regenerate the saved weekly/monthly digest of that trade automatically?
- The digest suggestion and norm availability still count the first trade date over all trades. A period with only excluded trades can be suggested and gives an empty strategy digest.
- An account whose only period trades are excluded has no per-account block in the digest; its trades appear in «Виключені угоди».
- Any status can be excluded, open positions included.
